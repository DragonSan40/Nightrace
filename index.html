NIGHT·RACE

"The night is our track; the dawn is our finish line."

Three Moods

Slow for an empty road at 3am, Cool for cruising with traffic, Hype for a touge run at redline. Pick whichever matches your night.

No Sign-Up

Press the ignition and go. No account, no login, nothing to set up first — just you and the road.

For Night Owls

Built for late sessions — long drives, late-night gaming, or just something keeping you company when the rest of the world's asleep.

What Night Race sounds like

A drive through city pop, synthwave and eurobeat-phonk, shifting with whichever mood you're in rather than sticking to one lane all night. Each track crossfades into the next instead of cutting — the road's speed and color shift with it, a live visualizer pulses with the music, and the whole thing opens with a real engine turning over. Rain and a handful of car sound effects round out the atmosphere, there if you want them, easy to ignore if you don't.

When to play it

  • Late-night drives or long gaming sessions
  • Solo work when you want momentum without lyrics pulling focus
  • Background energy for a late shift

How it works

  • Press Engine Start — the ignition sound is your cue
  • Pick a mode — Slow, Cool, or Hype; switching crossfades instead of cutting
  • Let it run — tracks advance and crossfade on their own, nothing to skip
  • Sit back — rain, car sounds, and the visualizer round out the ride
A note on credits: every track on Night Race is uploaded and credited directly through the Night Race admin panel. Rights remain with the original artists and labels — credits are kept current as the catalog changes.
