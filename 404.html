Night·Race

Privacy and music credits

Last updated 25 November 2026

In short: there are no accounts, no ads and no selling of anything. Night Race keeps a small amount of anonymous information about how it is used, including a rough city-level location. It never keeps your IP address itself, your name or your email.

Who runs this

Night Race is an independent project by Shivam Garg Shukla, powered by PACE Corp. To reach me, send a message to @sgs_40 on X.

What is collected

InformationWhy
A random ID, made on your deviceTo count listeners without knowing who they are. It is not linked to your name or email.
Device type, system, browser, screen size, language and time zoneTo make sure the player works on the devices people really use.
Approximate location (city and region, plus a rounded map position that is only accurate to about 10 km) and countryTo show where listeners are, on a private admin map and as a country total.
A scrambled one-way code made from your IP addressTo tell whether a visit comes from the same network, without keeping the address. It cannot be turned back into your IP address.
Plays, likes and dislikesTo show which songs are popular and to rank them.
Whether you are on the road right nowFor the live "racers online" count. This is held only while you are connected.

Not collected: your name, email, phone number, exact location, contacts, microphone or camera. Night Race never asks for these.

What stays on your device

Your volume, lane, favourites, recently played list, likes and dislikes, rain and sound settings, and your radio choice are saved in your browser's local storage so the app remembers you. They are not sent to me. Clearing your browser's site data for this site removes them, and removes your random ID too.

Who else sees some of it

Night Race is built from a few services, and each one handles the part it runs:

  • Supabase stores the anonymous records and runs the live "racers online" count.
  • Cloudflare stores and delivers the song files.
  • GitHub hosts the website files.
  • An IP lookup service (ipwho.is, with ipapi.co as a backup). To work out your approximate city, my server sends your IP address to one of these services for that single lookup. I then keep only the city, region and rounded position, not the address. Those services see your IP address, as any IP lookup does.
  • X is only involved if you choose "Send feedback on X" in the Garage. That link opens a post on X, and what you write there is covered by X's own policy, not this one. Night Race never sees it unless you post it.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typeface and jsDelivr supplies a code library. As with any website that uses them, they can see your IP address when your browser fetches them.

I do not sell or share this information with anyone else for advertising.

Who can read the records

Only I can, through a private admin page protected by a login. The public stats page shows totals only, such as plays, likes, countries and top songs, and never an individual listener.

How long it is kept

Records are kept while the project runs, and I clear out old records from time to time. If you would like something removed, see below.

Asking for removal

Because the records are anonymous, I usually cannot tell which one is yours. The most reliable step is to clear this site's data in your browser, which deletes your random ID from your device so it is not used again. If you would still like me to look into it, message me on X (above) and I will do what I can.

Music and copyright

I am replacing the song library with music that is free to share under Creative Commons licences or used with the artist's permission, and I credit each artist. If you are an artist or rights holder and one of your songs should not be here, or you would like a credit changed, message me on X with the song and I will remove or fix it quickly.

Changes

If what Night Race collects changes, I will update this page and the date at the top.
