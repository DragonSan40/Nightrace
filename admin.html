Song Fetch is your separate local downloader. This page just opens it and keeps the rules in front of you. Only bring in music that is public domain, CC0, CC-BY or that the artist has given written permission for. Avoid NC (non-commercial) and ND (no-derivatives) licences.

Open Song FetchPaste the address where Song Fetch runs on your computer (for example http://localhost:5000). It is remembered in this browser.

Before you add any song

  1. Open the track's own page and confirm the licence (CC0, CC-BY or public domain).
  2. Save the track page link and licence in your tracker sheet.
  3. Name the file Song Name - Artist.mp3 so the upload fills both fields.
  4. Upload it in the Tracks tab, then check it plays in the right lane.
